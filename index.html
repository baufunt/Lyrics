About

Lumo Bot

Lumo is a multi-purpose Discord bot built to make server management easy, fun, and powerful. Whether you're running a small friend group or a large community, Lumo has something for everyone.

With over 200 commands across 14 categories — from full economy systems and moderation tools to fun games and voice master — Lumo is the only bot you need.

Moderation

Full suite of tools including ban, kick, mute, warn, purge, auto-filter, and timeout management.

Economy

Complete economy with balance, daily rewards, jobs, gambling, shops, inventory, and robbery.

Ticket System

Professional support tickets with staff roles, claiming, transcripts, and full management.

Giveaways

Host giveaways with blacklist support, rerolls, and scheduled endings easily.

Voice Master

Dynamic voice channels that members can lock, limit, claim, and manage themselves.

Pokemon

Catch, duel, and collect Pokemon right in your Discord server with a full item and coin system.
