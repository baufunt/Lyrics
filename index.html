Getting started

Up and running in a few minutes.

1

Invite Solitary to your server

Click "Add to Discord" and authorize Solitary. Grant the recommended permissions so every feature works correctly.

2

Configure your setup

Use ,enable welcome #channel, ,modlogs enable #channel and ,setprefix to tailor Solitary to your server, or do it all from the Dashboard.

3

Build your first embed

Head to the Embed page, design your welcome message or announcement, and copy the generated command into Discord.

4

You're set

FAQ

Common questions, answered.

Is Solitary free to use?
Yes. All commands and features are available to every server, with no paid tier or subscription.
How do I change the bot prefix?
Use ,setprefix [new prefix]. The default is a comma, and each server can set its own.
What permissions does Solitary need?
Solitary requests Administrator so every feature works. Keep its role above others in your hierarchy for moderation to function correctly.
How do I use the Embed Builder?
Fill in the fields on the Embed page, watch the Discord preview update live, then copy the generated command and paste it into any channel Solitary is in.
Where do I get help or report a bug?
Join the support server and open a ticket — reports are tracked and answered quickly.
